# Ansai Core — AMENDMENTS.md
# Constitutional Change Record

Every change to AGENTS.md or CONTEXT.md is recorded here.
The constitution can evolve — but never silently.
Never without deliberate human ratification.
Never without a permanent record.

---

## Amendment categories

**Category 1 — Clarifications**
Small, local, low-risk changes.
Any engineer ratifies within their domain.
Format: engineer name · date · what changed · one sentence why.

**Category 2 — Extensions**
New rules added. New primitives registered.
New product codes assigned.
Senior engineer ratifies. Currently: Amasai Melchizedek.
Format: ratifier · date · what changed · full reasoning paragraph.

**Category 3 — Amendments**
Existing Tier 1 rules change. Defined terms redefined.
Non-negotiables modified. Governance model changed.
Requires consent from all founders.
Mandatory 48-hour minimum cooling-off before ratification.
7-day resolution window if consent is withheld.
After 7 days if consent still withheld, most senior
technical founder makes final call — dissent recorded.
Format: all ratifying parties · date · what changed ·
full reasoning · what superseded · dissenting views
if any · resolution.
